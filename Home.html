01 / About

In Process

I'm not trying to master fragrance.

I'm trying to understand it.


How can a single note shift a mood?

Why does clove feel mature, while citrus feels distant?

I'm still learning how notes translate into nuance,

and how nuance shapes identity.

How I Decode Scent

[ Blind ]

I don't read the notes first.

I try to identify what hits before anything influences me.

[ Compare ]

Does it remind me of something I’ve worn before?

I look for patterns.

[ Confirm ]

Only then I read the official notes.

Sometimes I’m right. Sometimes I’m not.
